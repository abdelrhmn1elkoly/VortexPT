FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

QUESTIONS, ANSWERED.

Everything you need to know about Vortex PT, file formats, and engineering licensing.

Vortex PT directly parses Bentley RAM Concept files (.cpt, .cpt8) from Concept v5 through v8 (including 2023, 2024, and 2025 releases) and ADAPT-Builder models (.adm) via paired companion exchange (.inb / .inp) files. It extracts tendon layout, tendon strand counts, jacking forces, and floor boundary coordinates with zero loss of engineering data.

Yes. Vortex PT generates native AutoCAD DWG drawings that can be opened directly in AutoCAD 2000 through 2026, Civil 3D, and BricsCAD without any conversion steps. All entities are mapped to clean engineering layers with true colors and dynamic self-measuring dimension styles.

Under standard post-tensioning practice, short tendons stressed from both ends suffer excessive friction and wedge seating loss at both jacks. Vortex PT features a configurable minimum length threshold (default 35.0 m). Any two-live-end tendon shorter than this threshold triggers an engineering alert, recommending one-end stressing instead.

No. Vortex PT operates 100% offline. License activation is cryptographically verified on your machine using an asymmetric Ed25519 digital signature bound to your hardware Machine ID. Your proprietary project models and engineering drawings never leave your computer.

Yes. The Settings tab lets you configure low chair deduction, high chair deduction, intermediate chair deductions, minimum chair height, and standard text height (default 200mm). The software also automatically formats chair height tables, separating Slab Chairs, Live End Chairs, and Dead End Chairs.
